    How to play

    Finish with the most coins. Everyone starts with 500.

    1. Read the bar

      A black bar hides one line of text. The ruler under it counts average-length words, and the black marker shows where the bar ends. A reading of 7.3 means about seven average words.

    2. Check the posted odds

      Four counts to choose from. Each shows its chance in % and a payout multiplier. The odds come only from the bar length at normal spacing. Rarer counts pay more, up to ×12.

    3. Catch the flicker

      Three times a round the bar turns see-through for a split second. The text underneath is blurred, so count the blobs.

    4. Wager and lock in

      Pick a wager (10, 25, 50, 100 or ALL), then tap a count. You have 15 seconds, and a faster lock-in earns a bigger bonus. Keys 1 to 4 also work.

    5. Watch the reveal

      The bar peels back and the words are counted one by one. A correct count returns your wager times the multiplier, plus speed and streak bonuses. A wrong count loses the wager.

    Mind the tracking

    Tracking is the spacing between letters. The odds assume normal spacing, so tight type hides extra words.

    • +40 Loose: fewer words fit
    • 0 Normal: the odds are fair
    • −45 Tight: more words fit
    • −80 Crushed: the most words fit

    Power-ups

    One of each per round, paid from your coins.

    • Scan −20: a longer glimpse under the bar
    • Tracking −20: shows the spacing for this round
    • 50/50 −40: removes two wrong counts

    Example

    The bar reads 7.3 and the options are:

    6 words 16%×6.37 words 43%×2.38 words 33%×3.09 words 8%×12.0

    You bet 50 on 8 words. If it is right, you get 150 back (+100 profit) plus bonuses. If it is wrong, you lose 50. If your coins run out, the house tops you up to 50.
